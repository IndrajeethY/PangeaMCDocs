## Earlier Patch Notes (2025)

### Patch Update — October 24, 2025

- Added `/sellhand` and `/sellall` commands
- Added Basic Kit (5-min cooldown) and PvP Starter Kit (1-hour cooldown)

### Custom Gears & Dungeons Expansion — October 9, 2025

- Introduced Custom Gears & Crafting Materials System
- Dungeon Expansion with Medium and Hard difficulties
- Tier-based crafting: 64 Iron Ingots = Tier 1 Material
- Boss drops, crate keys, and scrolls

### Weapon Forge Update — October 4, 2025

- Added Weapon Forge NPC at spawn
- Custom weapon gears and crafting materials obtainable through dungeons

### The Positivity Update — September 30, 2025

- New `/welcome` and `/wb` system — welcome players for 200 coins
- Welcome 5 in a row for 1,000 coin bonus
- Custom welcome messages with color codes

### Fishing Update — September 27, 2025

- Vanilla fishing replaced by custom system
- Rarer biomes = rarer fish
- `/fish catalog` and `/fish sell` commands

### Performance & Bug Fixes — September 26, 2025

- Bounties no longer drop in value when offline
- Custom Enchantment books overhauled
- Removed unused features for performance
- New envoy system

### Server Upgrade — September 9, 2025

- Migrated to new VPS for better ping and performance

### Early Access Launch — September 6, 2025

- PangeaMC launched with Survival RPG, custom skills, dungeons, 400+ enchants, shops, and more
