Linking your Discord account to your in-game profile gives you bonus rewards and lets you chat in-game directly from Discord.

## How to Link

1. Type `/discord link` in-game — you'll receive a code
2. DM the code to the **PangeaMC bot** on Discord
3. Done — your accounts are now linked

## Rewards

- **$10,000** in-game money
- **200 Coins** (spend in `/coinshop`)
- Access to **cross-chat** — talk to in-game players from Discord
- Access to linked-only Discord channels

<Callout type="warn" title="Important">
  Keep your Discord linked — it's required for account recovery if you ever forget your password.
</Callout>
