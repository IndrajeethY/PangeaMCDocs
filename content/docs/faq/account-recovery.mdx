---
title: Account Recovery
description: How to reset your password on PangeaMC if you get locked out.
---

Forgot your password? There are two ways to reset it, depending on what you've set up.

## Option 1 — Email Reset

If you've added your email with `/email add <email>` before:

1. Type `/email recover <your email>` in-game
2. Check your inbox (and spam/junk folder) for the reset link
3. Follow the link to set a new password

## Option 2 — Discord Reset

If your Discord account is [linked](/docs/faq/discord-linking):

1. DM the **PangeaMC bot** on Discord
2. Use the `/reset-password` command
3. Follow the bot's instructions — password resets instantly

<Callout type="warn" title="Set This Up Now">
  You **must** have both your email added (`/email add`) and Discord linked **before** you lose access. We cannot recover accounts without at least one of these set up.
</Callout>
