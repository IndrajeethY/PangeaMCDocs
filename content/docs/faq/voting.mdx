Voting helps the server grow and gives you free rewards every day. It takes about 30 seconds.

## How to Vote

1. Type `/vote` in-game to see all voting links
2. Open each link and vote for PangeaMC
3. Return in-game — rewards are given automatically

## Rewards (Per Vote)

- **1x Vote Crate Key** — open at spawn for random loot
- **$500** in-game money
- **5x Level XP**

Vote on all available sites to stack rewards. You can vote once per site per day.

## Voting Links

You can also vote directly from these links:

1. [play-minecraft-servers.com](https://play-minecraft-servers.com/minecraft-servers/pangeamc/?tab=vote)
2. [minecraft-serverlist.com](https://minecraft-serverlist.com/server/5131)

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Make voting part of your daily routine — the crate keys alone are worth it. You'll also help new players discover the server.
</Callout>
