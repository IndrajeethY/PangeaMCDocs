title: Server Rules
description: Official PangeaMC server rules — PvP, fair play, trading, building, and account policies.
---

Please read carefully to ensure fair and fun gameplay on our Survival Server.

## PvP & Combat

- PvP is allowed in all worlds **except** claimed areas
- Be cautious when using `/tpa` — meet in `/spawn` or safe zones for trades
- You accept full responsibility if you die after teleporting
- PvP traps are **strictly prohibited** — no unfair advantage traps

## Fair Play

- **No cheats, hacks, or exploit abuse** (including Auto-Clickers)
- Report bugs immediately — exploiting bugs leads to punishment
- No alt account abuse for unfair benefits
- Use common sense and don't abuse loopholes

## Accounts Policy

- Each player is allowed **one main account and one alt** (2 total)
- Using more than one alt, sharing accounts, or evading punishments = ban on all accounts

## Trading & Economy

- **No scamming** in the Auction House (`/ah`)
- Trade only via Auction House — trades outside `/ah` are at your own risk
- Staff cannot assist with scams outside official systems

## Building & Claims

- **Claim your base** properly to protect it
- Griefed builds won't be restored if trust was misplaced
- Unclaimed builds won't be restored
- **LavaCasts are strictly prohibited**

## No Lag Machines

Any contraptions intentionally made to cause server lag will result in an instant ban.

## English-Only Chat

Please use English in public chat so everyone can communicate. You can use other languages in private messages.

## No Duplication or Exploits

Duping items or abusing bugs/exploits is an instant ban. If you find a glitch, **report it to staff** instead of abusing it.

## Account & Technical

- Switching clients/modes or using VPNs can reset your UUID
- Lost items/progress from UUID reset won't be restored
- No item restoration for bugs, lag, or inventory overflow

<Callout type="warn" title="Important Reminders">
- **Read and follow the rules** — "I didn't know" is not an excuse
- **Staff decisions are final** — respect their rulings
- **Be respectful** to all players and staff members
- **Have fun** — that's what we're here for!
</Callout>

## Punishments

Breaking rules may result in:

1. **Warning** — First offense for minor violations
2. **Temporary Mute** — Chat violations
3. **Temporary Ban** — Repeated or serious violations
4. **Permanent Ban** — Severe violations (hacking, major exploits, toxicity)

Appeals can be submitted through the [Discord server](https://dsc.gg/pangeamc).
