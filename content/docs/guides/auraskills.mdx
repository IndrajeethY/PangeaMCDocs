<Callout type="info" title="The Key Thing to Know">
  **Just play the game.** Every action you take — mining, fighting, farming, fishing, even walking — passively levels up your character. You don't need to do anything special.
</Callout>

PangeaMC has a skill system that tracks what you do and rewards you with permanent stat bonuses. The more you play, the stronger your character becomes.

## How It Works

Everything is automatic. When you mine a block, your Mining skill goes up. When you fight a mob, your Combat skills go up. When you harvest crops, your Farming skill goes up. You'll see XP notifications as you play.

As your skills level up, your **stats** increase — things like extra health, more damage, faster mining, better luck for rare drops, and more.

## Skill Categories

**Combat** — Fighting, Archery, Defense. Level up by dealing and taking damage. You'll hit harder and survive longer.

**Gathering** — Mining, Farming, Foraging, Fishing, Excavation. Level up by collecting resources. You'll get double drops, faster gathering, and rare finds.

**Utility** — Agility, Endurance, Alchemy, Enchanting, Healing, Sorcery. Level up through movement, brewing, enchanting, and more. You'll gain speed, health, and ability buffs.

## Checking Your Progress

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/skills` | View all your skill levels |
| `/stats` | See your current stat bonuses |
| `/skill <name>` | Details about a specific skill |
| `/abtop` | Server-wide skill leaderboards |

That's it. No complicated setup, no grinding guides — just play PangeaMC and watch your character grow.
