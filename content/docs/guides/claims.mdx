---
title: Protecting Your Builds
description: How to claim land, manage permissions, and keep your builds safe on PangeaMC.
---

Everything you build on PangeaMC can be protected with land claims. Once claimed, nobody can break your blocks, open your chests, or mess with your stuff.

<Callout type="warn" title="Always Claim First">
  Unclaimed builds will **not** be restored if griefed. Claim your land before you start building.
</Callout>

## How to Claim Land

You need a **golden shovel** — get one from `/kits`.

1. Hold the golden shovel and **right-click one corner** of the area you want to protect
2. Walk to the **opposite corner** and right-click again
3. Done — gold blocks and glowstone will show your claim boundaries

You can also just stand in your build area and type **`/claim`** to auto-claim around you.

You earn claim blocks by playing on the server. The more you play, the more land you can protect. Check your available blocks with `/claims`.

## Essential Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/claim` | Create a claim where you're standing |
| `/claims` | View and manage all your claims |
| `/trust <player>` | Give a player full access to build in your claim |
| `/untrust <player>` | Remove a player's access |
| `/abandonclaim` | Delete the claim you're standing in (gives blocks back) |

## Sharing With Friends

Want to let a friend build in your base? Stand inside your claim and type:

```
/trust <their name>
```

They'll be able to build, break blocks, and open chests. If you change your mind:

```
/untrust <their name>
```

Use `/trustlist` while standing in your claim to see who currently has access.

## Tips

- **Claim first, build second.** Always. No exceptions.
- **Check your claim blocks** with `/claims` — you earn more by playing.
- **Don't trust strangers** — only give access to people you actually know.
- **Abandon old claims** with `/abandonclaim` to free up blocks for new builds.
- Voting and events can give you bonus claim blocks.
