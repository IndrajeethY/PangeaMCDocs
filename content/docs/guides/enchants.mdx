## How to Get Them

Custom enchantments show up the same way as vanilla ones:

- **Enchanting table** — Custom enchantments can appear alongside normal ones
- **Loot chests** — Dungeons, structures, and crates can drop enchanted books
- **Fishing** — Rare chance to fish up custom enchantment books
- **Villagers** — Librarian villagers may sell custom enchant books
- **Auction House** — Buy them from other players with `/ah`

Apply them using an **anvil**, just like any enchantment book.

## Universal Enchantments

These can go on any item:

| Enchantment | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Restore | Saves your item from breaking once |
| Soulbound | Item stays with you on death |
| Curse of Breaking | Item loses durability faster |
| Curse of Fragility | Item can't be repaired at anvil or grindstone |
| Curse of Mediocrity | Removes enchantments from block/mob drops |
| Curse of Misfortune | Reduces drops to zero |

<Callout type="warn" title="Watch Out for Curses">
  Curse enchantments have powerful downsides. Check the tooltip carefully before applying — they **cannot be removed** once on an item.
</Callout>
