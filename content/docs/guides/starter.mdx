Welcome to PangeaMC — an RPG Survival Minecraft server with custom skills, 80+ unique enchantments, a handcrafted world, and an active community. This guide walks you through your first day.

## Step 1: You Just Spawned

You'll spawn in our server hub. Here's what to do first:

1. **Grab your starter kit** — Type `/kits` and claim the starter kit. It gives you basic tools, food, and a golden shovel (you'll need this later).
2. **Claim your daily rewards** — Type `/rewards` to see what's available. You get better rewards the more days in a row you log in.

## Step 2: Find a Spot to Build

You need to get out into the wild and find somewhere to call home.

- **`/rtp`** — Teleports you to a random safe location in the wilderness. Great for finding untouched land.
- **`/warps`** — Shows key server locations like the shop, arena, and resource areas.
- **`/spawn`** — Takes you back to the hub anytime.

Once you find a spot you like, move to the next step right away — don't build without claiming first!

## Step 3: Claim and Protect Your Base

<Callout type="warn" title="Claim Before You Build">
  Unclaimed builds **will not be restored** if they get griefed. Always claim your land first.
</Callout>

1. **Equip your golden shovel** (from the starter kit)
2. **Right-click one corner** of the area you want to protect
3. **Walk to the opposite corner** and right-click again
4. Your claim is set! Gold blocks and glowstone will mark the boundaries.

You can also just type **`/claim`** while standing where you want to protect.

Now **set your home** so you can always get back:
- **`/sethome`** — Saves your current location
- **`/home`** — Teleports you back anytime

You can set multiple homes with `/sethome base`, `/sethome farm`, etc. Use `/homes` to see them all.

<Callout type="info" title="Want the full claiming guide?">
  Check out [Protecting Your Builds](/docs/guides/claims) for trust permissions, claim management, and tips.
</Callout>

## Step 4: Make Money

The server has an economy. Here's how to start earning:

- **`/shop`** — Open the server shop. Buy essentials or sell your extra resources for cash.
- **`/ah`** — The player Auction House. Browse what others are selling, or list your own items with `/ah sell <price>`.
- **`/vote`** — Vote for the server on listing sites to earn vote crate keys, $500, and XP. [Do this daily!](/docs/faq/voting)
- **`/balance`** — Check how much money you have.

## Step 5: Meet People

PangeaMC is better with friends.

- **`/tpa <player>`** — Request to teleport to someone. They accept with `/tpaccept`.
- **`/msg <player> <message>`** — Send a private message.
- **Join our [Discord](https://dsc.gg/pangeamc)** — Chat with the community, get help, and stay updated on events.
- **`/discord link`** — [Link your Discord](/docs/faq/discord-linking) to get **$10,000 + 200 coins** and cross-chat access.
- **`/guild create <name>`** — Start a [guild](/docs/faq/guilds) with your friends for shared homes and private chat.

## What Makes PangeaMC Special

**Custom Skills** — Everything you do levels up your character. Mine, fight, farm, fish — you passively unlock stat bonuses and abilities just by playing. Check `/skills` anytime. [Learn more →](/docs/guides/auraskills)

**80+ Custom Enchantments** — Beyond vanilla enchantments, you'll find things like Veinminer, Smelter, Treefeller, Vampire, and way more. Find them in loot, through enchanting, or on the auction house. [Learn more →](/docs/guides/enchants)

**Handcrafted World** — Our world is custom-generated with 15+ unique biomes, from Cherry Groves to Gorges to Scarlet Forests, plus custom structures like Catacombs and enhanced Villages across a 15k x 15k map.

**Bounty System** — Place bounties on other players or hunt them yourself for rewards. Use `/bounty` to see active targets. PvP is enabled in the open world (but you're safe inside your claim!). [Learn more →](/docs/faq/bounty)

**Skins** — Change your character's look anytime with `/skin <name>` or browse the GUI with `/skins`. [Learn more →](/docs/faq/skins)

## Important: Set Up Account Recovery

<Callout type="warn" title="Do This Now — Before You Forget">
  Set up your email and Discord link so you can recover your account if you ever lose access.

  1. **`/email add <your email>`** — Register your email for password resets
  2. **`/discord link`** — Link your Discord account ([full guide](/docs/faq/discord-linking))

  Without these, **we cannot recover your account**. [Learn more →](/docs/faq/account-recovery)
</Callout>
